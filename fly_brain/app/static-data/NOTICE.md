# MaleCNS-derived data

Source: https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/
Dataset: male-cns:v1.0. License: CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Attribution: FlyEM / HHMI Janelia, University of Cambridge, MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, Google Research.

This distribution contains transformed soma positions and neuron labels, aggregated circuit populations and precomputed model outputs. It is not an unmodified release of the original dataset.
The coordinate transform is recorded in index.json. The graph includes annotated bodies and excludes connections to unannotated segmentation fragments. Population activity and body posture are derived simulations, not measured physiology.
Hashes in manifest.json describe this distribution. The original acquisition date and source revision beyond the v1.0 designation are not recorded here.
