tracts.f32 — white-matter tractography used in the home-page DTI visualization
=============================================================================

Source dataset:
  Population-based (HCP-1065) tractography atlas of the human brain.
  Built from 1065 Human Connectome Project subjects' diffusion-MRI tractograms,
  in ICBM-2009a Nonlinear Asymmetric space.

Citation:
  Yeh, Fang-Ching. "Population-based tract-to-region connectome of the human
  brain and its hierarchical topology." Nature Communications 13, 4933 (2022).
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32595-4
  Atlas: https://brain.labsolver.org/hcp_trk_atlas.html

License:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Derivative note:
  tracts.f32 is a derivative of the above atlas: streamlines were point-subsampled
  and spatially normalized (centered + scaled), then stored as flat little-endian
  Float32 triplets with a NaN,NaN,NaN separator between streamlines. As a
  derivative of a CC BY-SA 4.0 work, this file is likewise released under
  CC BY-SA 4.0.
